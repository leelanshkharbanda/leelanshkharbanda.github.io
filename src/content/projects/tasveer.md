## Overview

Tasveer is IIIT Delhi's media society. I serve as an event lead and coordinate the society's static archive site, which organizes campus photo essays under **THE ROLL** and credits photographers, editors, writers, and developers under **THE CREW**.

## Source

Code lives in [tasveer](https://github.com/leelanshkharbanda/tasveer) and the live site is hosted at [leelanshkharbanda.github.io/tasveer](https://leelanshkharbanda.github.io/tasveer/).
