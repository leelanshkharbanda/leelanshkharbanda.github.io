# Council Review 05: Factual Consistency

Date: 2026-11-25

## Findings

- Life OS and Modulation-Studio were incorrectly called individual projects in the content review.
- Several team records omitted verified team sizes.
- Chronos was described with an unsupported software/hardware boundary.
- The résumé route was inconsistently written as `/resume` and `/resume/`.

## Fixes

- Life OS is labeled team prototype (3 people); Modulation-Studio is team project (2 people).
- HPC_Boost is team research (2), RISC-V is team project (4), AP_Project is coordinated team work (2), and MoE is group coursework.
- Chronos is framed as geometric-channel software simulation, not hardware measurement or calibrated RF.
- `/resume/` is canonical in the council records.
- The content schema now requires verified team size when available and explicit contribution statements.

## Disposition

The corrected records are suitable for council adoption, subject to the pending award-directory differentiation check and implementation verification.
