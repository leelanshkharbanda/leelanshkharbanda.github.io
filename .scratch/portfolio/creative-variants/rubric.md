# Personal Portfolio Creative Variant Council

## Question

Which direction makes Leelansh's projects feel like a personal showcase—curious, made-by-a-person, and fun to explore—without reading like a consultancy or senior-engineer sales page?

## Branches

- `variant/project-playground` — tactile, kinetic project playground.
- `variant/personal-scrapbook` — notebook/corkboard of things built, broken, and learned.
- `variant/after-dark-arcade` — late-night project lab and selection screen.

## Scoring

| Axis | Weight | What the council looks for |
|---|---:|---|
| Personality and authorship | 25 | First-person voice, visible interests, humor, warmth, no services pitch |
| Project discovery and storytelling | 25 | Projects are the hero; easy to compare, open, and understand |
| Interaction quality | 20 | Motion has meaning, invites play, works with keyboard/touch, respects reduced motion |
| Visual originality | 15 | Structurally distinct, not a recolor or generic card grid, no copied assets |
| Usability and readability | 15 | Clear navigation, legible typography, mobile reflow, source/evidence labels remain visible |

## Hard penalties

- Corporate/services language, “hire me,” rate cards, client logos, or availability claims.
- Project facts hidden behind animation or interaction.
- Copied reference code, assets, prose, branding, or recognizable hero composition.
- Broken core navigation, unreadable mobile layout, or inaccessible-only interactions.

## Deliberately de-emphasized

Payload and micro-optimization are secondary for this low-traffic personal showcase. Basic performance sanity still matters, but personality and project exploration win.

## Decision rule

The council may select one branch or combine specific strengths. The selected direction must have a clear verdict, reasons, rejected alternatives, and a short improvement backlog before folding into main.
