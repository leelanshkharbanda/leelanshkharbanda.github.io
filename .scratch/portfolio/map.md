## Destination

A verified evidence and constraint foundation for an implementation-ready portfolio scope, with human-preference decisions explicitly handed to the user-authorized council workflow.

## Notes

- Domain: personal engineering portfolio at the software/hardware intersection.
- The user is unavailable and explicitly delegated unresolved choices to the review council defined in [Council Charter](council.md).
- Consult `GLOSSARY.md`, `outputs/github-evidence.md`, and the technical-portfolio research outputs before deciding.
- Applicable skills: `research` for external evidence, `domain-modeling` for terminology, and `writing-for-agents` for agent-facing documents. Wayfinder `prototype` and `grilling` tickets remain HITL and are not resolved by agents.
- This map scopes evidence and constraints; implementation follows after the route is clear and the separate council decisions are recorded.
- Preserve honest distinctions between individual and team work, simulation and measurement, and original work and forks.
- Research context pointers link directly to local artifacts because this repository began without a baseline commit or remote; branch/push provenance is deferred until the user authorizes commits and a remote exists.

## Decisions so far

- [Establish the evidence boundary](issues/01-evidence-boundary.md): original repositories are the source of truth; forks, unsupported outcomes, and unlicensed media are excluded.
