# Council Decision: Define the Essential Experience

Status: resolved
Blocked by: —

## Question

What sections, project depth, navigation, contact actions, and content hierarchy are necessary for a strong first visit, while avoiding speculative features?

## Decision

Use the journey **orient → select relevant evidence → inspect → verify → contact**.

- Home: orientation hero, exactly three selected projects (Chronos-DSP, Life OS, Modulation-Studio), short About preview, contact, footer.
- Work: five case studies (Chronos-DSP, Life OS, Modulation-Studio, HPC_Boost, MoE-Memory-Wall) plus a three-item archive (RISC-V, AP_Project, tasveer).
- Case study: question → at-a-glance credit/evidence → context/constraints → mechanism → contribution → artifact → results ledger → limits → sources/next step.
- About: verified identity, recurring themes, toolkit, working principles, contact.
- Résumé: canonical `/resume/`, text-first and print-friendly, generated from the same facts.
- Contact: visible institutional email, `mailto:`, copy-email enhancement, GitHub, LinkedIn. No form, backend, tracker, or availability claim.
- Exclude forks as original work, unlicensed media, unsupported metrics, search/filters, CMS, 3D spectacle, scroll hijacking, custom cursor, intro gates, and autoplay audio.

## Acceptance criteria

See [Content and UX Review 03](../council-reviews/03-content-and-ux.md), corrected by [Factual Consistency Review 05](../council-reviews/05-factual-consistency.md). Team sizes and simulation/measurement labels are release-blocking content invariants.
