# Council Decision: Choose the Portfolio Signal

Status: resolved
Blocked by: —

## Question

Which creative direction best turns the verified project inventory into a memorable, coherent portfolio without eclipsing the engineering evidence?

## Decision

Adopt **Indexed Proof Sheet**, the final refinement of Evidence Bench: warm paper between carbon-black evidence rails, signal-red index rules, trace-teal data paths, expressive grotesk typography, monospaced datum labels, asymmetric folio rows, and a claim-to-source system.

Rejected alternatives:

- **Systems Atlas:** stronger for architecture projects but weaker for signal phenomena and too close to spatial creative-developer portfolios.
- **Workshop Ledger:** strong decision-log candor but reads as a personal notebook before an engineering portfolio.

## Final differentiation adjustments

- Use a continuous carbon datum gutter and 4 px signal-red index rule; paper is the reading surface between ink rails.
- Treat the three-font stack as a measured-subset candidate set; fall back to two web-font families plus system mono if the 60 KB cap requires it.
- Default display composition targets 5.5 rem and two lines, but headings must grow and wrap under zoom/text-spacing overrides.
- Use folio rows rather than boxed cards, thumbnail walls, or decorative grids.
- Replace the split hero with identity and positioning above a static three-datum proof strip: question → artifact → evidence class/limit.
- Keep the Trace Inspector only in Chronos-DSP, framed as four evidence-ledger rows with Previous/Next controls and no autoplay.

## Acceptance criteria

See [Creative Review 02](../council-reviews/02-creative-direction.md) and [Award Differentiation Review 06](../council-reviews/06-award-differentiation.md). The final implementation must preserve datum-linked evidence, static/no-JS equivalents, 320 px reflow, 44 px primary targets, reduced motion, and the performance budgets.
