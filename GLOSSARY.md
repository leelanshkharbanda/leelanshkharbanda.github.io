# Engineering Portfolio

A personal portfolio that makes Leelansh Kharbanda’s original software and hardware-adjacent engineering work understandable through evidence, interaction, and precise narrative.

## Language

**Project Evidence**:
A verifiable artifact from an original repository, such as source code, tests, a release, or a live demo, that supports a portfolio claim.
_Avoid_: proof, hype, unspecified accomplishments

**Engineering Narrative**:
A project story structured around the problem, constraints, mechanism, evidence, and limits.
_Avoid_: feature list, sales pitch

**Simulation Result**:
An outcome produced by a model under stated assumptions; it is not a measurement from physical hardware.
_Avoid_: benchmark win, measured speedup

**Measured Result**:
An outcome observed from physical hardware or an external real-world measurement dataset under reproducible conditions. Model-generated outputs are Simulation Results.
_Avoid_: simulation result

**Signal Path**:
The sequence through which a signal is generated, modulated, impaired by a channel, and recovered by a receiver.
_Avoid_: generic pipeline

**Channel Model**:
A defined abstraction of propagation effects used to explore communication behavior.
_Avoid_: calibrated RF model, production radio

**Hardware Trace**:
A recorded sequence of hardware or system events used as experimental evidence.
_Avoid_: raw benchmark

**Memory System**:
The modeled or implemented movement, caching, and replacement of data and expert weights under a resource budget.
_Avoid_: storage feature

**Local-first Software**:
Software whose core data and useful operation remain available locally, even when optional network integrations exist.
_Avoid_: fully offline

**Collaboration Credit**:
An explicit distinction between individual contribution, team contribution, and upstream work.
_Avoid_: implied sole authorship

**Live Artifact**:
A working project demo or visual evidence that can be opened or interacted with directly.
_Avoid_: screenshot-only proof

**Portfolio Signal**:
The recurring visual and interaction idea that connects the portfolio’s sections without obscuring the project evidence.
_Avoid_: theme, decoration
